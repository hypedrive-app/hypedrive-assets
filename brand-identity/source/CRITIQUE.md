# Cleaner and more polished: per-page fixes (lead review, 2026-11-26)

Owner: "cleaner and more polished pages". The pages already use the design system (see EVOLVE.md, MATURITY.md). This pass removes noise and tightens the craft. Do the listed fixes, then look for more of the same kind on your pages.

## Rules behind every fix
- **A badge or pill must not repeat what the heading, title or meta already says.** "Your turn" plus a "Submit tasks" badge plus "Submit 2 tasks" says it three times. Keep only exception badges (Fix tasks, Paused, Void, Sent back, 9 to review).
- **Normal states carry no badge:** To do, Sent, Paid, Joined, Live, Verified.
- **No grey slabs.** Chips, role selectors and segment options are white with a hairline (the kit chip / seg); the selected one gets the accent-subtle fill.
- **Every block keeps generous, even spacing.** Use 12 between cards, 20–24 between sections, and nothing cramped against a sticky bar (add bottom padding so the last row clears it).
